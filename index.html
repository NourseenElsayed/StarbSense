Interpretation and limitations

Complete a session to generate its summary.

StarbSense is an experimental prototype. These experimental measurements are not clinically validated and cannot diagnose or rule out strabismus or any other eye condition.

Privacy and responsible use

Camera processing is performed in the browser. Session CSV files contain derived facial landmark coordinates; handle them carefully, especially when working with children.
