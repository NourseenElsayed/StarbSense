How these models relate to StarbSense

These are offline image experiments. They accept pre-cropped eye-pair images; the current browser session records MediaPipe landmarks and experimental image measurements. The image models are not connected to the webcam stream and do not analyze the session CSV.

Accuracy on these datasets does not establish performance on real-world webcam recordings. These experimental models must not be used to diagnose or rule out an eye condition or decide whether someone needs medical care.

UNDERSTANDING YOUR SESSION

How to read your result

There are two different outputs: the camera-session report describes recording quality, while the optional image experiment predicts a dataset label for one uploaded image.

Camera-session result This shows whether enough usable frames and eye landmarks were recorded. It is not a strabismus classification.
Image-model result This shows the model’s predicted label for the uploaded image: “strabismus” or “strabismus-free”, or one of the five image classes.

These are research outputs, not a medical diagnosis.

STARBSENSE GUIDE · LOCAL ASSISTANT

Step 4 · Ask StarbSense

Ask about your session, charts, image-model labels, camera quality, or how StarbSense works. The assistant uses the project data available in this browser.

Medical next steps

StarbSense measurements alone cannot decide whether an eye examination is needed. If you have a concern about vision or eye alignment, arrange an eye-care assessment.

Ask about your session summary, camera quality, charts, model labels, or the test steps.

For questions outside the project, the assistant may not have enough information.

Privacy and responsible use

Camera processing is performed in the browser. Session CSV files contain derived facial landmark coordinates; handle them carefully, especially when working with children.
