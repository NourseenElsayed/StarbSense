STRUCTURED RESEARCH SESSION · V2

A planned path, not random movement

The star follows a fixed sequence of center, horizontal, vertical, and diagonal screen targets. This provides a repeatable experimental stimulus path; its positions and timings are design parameters, not clinical test standards.

17 FIXATION STEPS
01 · Center 02 · Left ↔ Right 03 · Up ↕ Down 04 · Diagonals 05 · Center

This sequence is an experimental visual-following task. The star shape is an engagement choice, and the directions are used to sample screen locations. Neither the shape, path, nor timing is clinically validated. This is not a cover test, calibrated gaze test, or validated strabismus screening protocol.

How these models relate to StarbSense

These are offline image experiments. They accept pre-cropped eye-pair images; the current browser session records MediaPipe landmarks and experimental image measurements. The image models are not connected to the webcam stream and do not analyze the session CSV.

Accuracy on these datasets does not establish performance on real-world webcam recordings. These experimental models must not be used to diagnose or rule out an eye condition or decide whether someone needs medical care.

UNDERSTANDING YOUR SESSION

What should I do with this result?

StarbSense reports experimental tracking and data-quality measurements only. It cannot decide whether someone's eyes are healthy, diagnose or rule out strabismus, or determine that an eye examination is unnecessary.

If there is an eye-related concern Ask a parent or guardian to arrange a comprehensive examination with an eye-care professional, especially if an eye appears to turn, vision seems different between the eyes, or double vision occurs.
If symptoms begin suddenly Sudden new double vision, a sudden change in eye alignment, or sudden vision loss needs urgent medical assessment.

A reassuring-looking StarbSense session does not replace routine eye care or a professional examination.

Medical next steps

StarbSense measurements alone cannot decide whether an eye examination is needed. If you have a concern about vision or eye alignment, arrange an eye-care assessment.

You can ask about the session summary, data quality, the star-following protocol, privacy, or when to seek professional eye care.

This assistant uses a limited local knowledge base and simple rules. It does not understand every possible question, provide a diagnosis, or replace a clinician.

Privacy and responsible use

Camera processing is performed in the browser. Session CSV files contain derived facial landmark coordinates; handle them carefully, especially when working with children.
